# Reign in Wealth Retreats — design direction

## Positioning

A distinct international travel and retreat brand within The Millionaire Chick family. The experience begins with the appeal of a beautifully curated journey and develops into the brand’s differentiators: meaningful relationships, cultural discovery, purpose, faith, and exposure to global opportunities.

The site should feel considered and welcoming. It should not resemble a crowded travel marketplace, investment sales funnel, or template with invented proof.

## Visual system

- **Emerald:** structure, primary actions, and intimate brand sections.
- **Warm ivory:** generous reading space and the main canvas.
- **Gold:** restrained accents inspired by the supplied logo.
- **Cormorant Garamond:** expressive editorial headings, with occasional italic emphasis.
- **DM Sans:** readable body copy, concise navigation, and dependable form labels.
- **Photography:** real images of the named destinations, with a Zanzibar coastline as the main scenic introduction. Ghana’s architectural landmark gives the destination collection visual variety.
- **Icons:** consistent outlined Lucide icons; no emoji interface elements.

The original logo is retained. “Retreats” and the parent-brand relationship provide the business context without inventing a separate identity.

## Information architecture

1. Homepage: brand promise, destination discovery, experience pillars, founder context, journal invitation, FAQs, and community signup.
2. Retreat collection: the four announced destinations and clear availability states.
3. Destination pages: overview, dates, gallery, proposed or confirmed itinerary, accommodation status, package details, FAQs, and the appropriate next step.
4. Experience: Explore, Connect, Invest, Experience, Transform, and Reign.
5. Founder: Kimona Hanson’s role and mission as supplied in the brief.
6. Past retreats: a visual journal framework beginning with Ghana 2026, awaiting authentic media.
7. Testimonials: a home for consented traveler feedback, with honest empty states until published.
8. FAQs and contact: reduce uncertainty and route individual, group, and partnership inquiries.
9. Reservation and traveler portal: one registration per person, clear occupancy preference, payment schedule, and balance.
10. Administration: content, dates, pricing, bookings, rooms, leads, media, messages, promotions, and updates.

## Content integrity

The supplied brief confirms the destination names and these travel dates:

- Dubai: December 27, 2026 to January 2, 2027.
- Ghana: August 5–15, 2027.
- Zanzibar: September 2027, exact dates to be confirmed.
- Dominican Republic: coming soon.

It does not confirm package prices, deposits, seat counts, hotels, final daily activities, cancellation rules, contact details, social URLs, traveler reviews, or a founder portrait. Those fields therefore remain unconfigured. Dubai and Ghana contain expressly proposed daily outlines to help the owner develop the final program. Zanzibar and Dominican Republic have no invented trip duration.

The site supports interest capture before reservations open. Payment eligibility is controlled separately from publishing a destination. Staff should confirm the actual package and approve the business policies before enabling paid reservations.

Investment exposure is described as educational and exploratory. The copy does not promise a return, guarantee a property purchase, or imply that every retreat includes a confirmed investment opportunity.

## Motion and accessibility

Use restrained fades and image movement only where they help the composition. Respect reduced-motion preferences, keep visible keyboard focus, provide image alternatives, retain explicit field labels, and use status text in addition to color. Media controls should be operable by keyboard and accessible touch targets should remain comfortably sized.
